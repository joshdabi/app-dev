# My Favorite Movie: The Raid 2

## Why I love it
**The Raid 2** is an Indonesian action film. It is a direct sequel to *The Raid: Redemption* and takes the intense martial arts and fight choreography to a much larger, criminal underworld scale and they DO NOT shy away from showing brutal scenes. It is why its one of my favorites and I personally prefer it over any MCU movie.

### Key Highlights 
* **Director:** Gareth Evans
* **Main Actor:** Iko Uwais (as Rama)
* **Action Style:** Pencak Silat
* **Best Scene:** Prison Mud Fight in my opinion. Everyone is doing something and is part of the action.

> "It's not just a martial arts movie; it's a sprawling crime epic."

If you want to learn more about it, check out the [IMDb page for The Raid 2](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2265171/).
